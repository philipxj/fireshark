Studio

Less noise.
More utility.

Fireshark Tech is built around a simple product philosophy: solve a concrete problem, keep the experience clear, and ship on the platforms where the product is genuinely useful.

01

Focused by default

Every product has a clear primary job. Features should make that job easier, not turn a small tool into a crowded platform.

02

Platform-aware

Mobile, web, and browser experiences are designed around what each platform does well instead of forcing one interface everywhere.

03

Built for real use

Distribution, privacy, support, compatibility, and day-to-day reliability are treated as product work, not as an afterthought.
